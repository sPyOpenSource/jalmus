Falling hint bar — animation style

A hint bar falls onto the correct piano key to show which computer key to press (learning mode, note reading only). Choose the animation style.

A. Fast fall from top of window

Bar starts at the very top of the window and drops quickly onto the key, landing just before the note should be pressed. High urgency, clear gesture.

B. Gentle fall from above the staff

Bar starts just above the musical staff and drifts down onto the piano key. Slower, more relaxed — suits a learning/practice pace.

C. Instant highlight (no fall)

No animation — the correct piano key simply glows/lights up when the note appears. Simplest to implement, least distracting.

D. Falling bar that pulses on landing

Bar falls from above the staff, lands on the key, then pulses (flash) until the user presses the matching computer key or the note changes.
